# Health and Recognition Notice

FoodLens provides general food-label education. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Check the original packaging

Recognition may miss or misread ingredients, nutrition values, units, serving sizes, or allergen statements. Glare, blur, small print, and incomplete photos affect results. Missing values are not zero, and no warning does not mean a food is safe. Check the original label and manufacturer information before eating.

## Allergies and medical restrictions

The app's allergen list is incomplete and cannot rule out cross-contact or undisclosed risks. Do not rely solely on FoodLens when you have a severe allergy.

Reminders about blood glucose, blood lipids, blood pressure, or other dietary concerns are general education, not permission to eat a food with a medical condition. Follow individualized professional advice for diagnosed conditions, pregnancy, medication, or medically required dietary restrictions.

## Portions and reference values

Per-serving, per-100-gram, and whole-package calculations depend on recognized data and your selected amount. Daily-value comparisons use the reference identified on the results screen, such as U.S. FDA adult Daily Values, rather than an individual dietary target. National labeling systems may differ.

## Examples and cloud results

Example products and data are fictional. Real label photos are read by Cloudflare Workers AI. The app does not claim clinical validation or a guaranteed accuracy rate. The current app is free and has no subscription.
