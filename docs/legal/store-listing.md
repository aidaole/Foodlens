## Full description

Make food labels easier to understand with FoodLens. Take a clear photo of a package's ingredients or nutrition panel, crop the label, and explore what it says.

- Read ingredients and label evidence from your photo.
- Explore calories, total fat, saturated fat, protein, carbohydrates, fiber, sugar, and sodium when shown on the label.
- Compare amounts per serving, per 100 g or 100 mL, and per package when the required label information is available.
- Choose the nutrients you want to focus on, and set dietary and allergy reminders locally.
- Revisit recent scans with compressed label thumbnails and save favorites on your device.
- Try clearly labeled example products without sending a photo.

Cloud recognition requires an internet connection and your agreement to send the cropped image to Cloudflare Workers AI. Recognition is subject to service limits. Dietary settings and history stay on your device; FoodLens does not currently provide cloud history synchronization or require an account.

Recognition may miss or misread small print, allergens, serving sizes, and values. Missing information is not zero, and no warning is not proof of safety. Always check original packaging and manufacturer information.

FoodLens is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The app does not determine whether a food is safe for a medical condition or a severe allergy.
