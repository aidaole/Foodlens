## Monthly plan

FoodLens Pro renews monthly. The proposed reference price is CNY 9.90. Overseas stores display their actual localized currency and price; the purchase screen and store confirmation identify the applicable price, currency, billing period and taxes. This plan has no annual option or free subscription trial.

Each anonymous free-use identity receives three successful recognitions in total, without a monthly reset. An active monthly subscription includes 50 successful recognitions per subscription period. A successful renewal starts a new period. Unused recognitions do not carry over or have cash value. Examples use no allowance. Failed recognitions and retake requests do not consume plan allowance.

The server records usage, so deleting local photos or history does not reset it. An already-sent request can count if it completes successfully on the server, even if your phone loses its connection, you stop waiting, or you do not receive the result. Service unavailability does not consume plan allowance. Contact support if a completed request consumed allowance without delivering a result.

## Purchase and activation

Apple or Google processes payment through its in-app purchase system. FoodLens does not receive full payment-card details. Access begins after store payment confirmation and backend verification. Pending approval, failed payments and canceled purchases do not activate a subscription. Do not purchase again merely because a confirmation is delayed.

## Renewal, cancellation and refunds

The store renews and charges your subscription unless you cancel by its applicable deadline. Renewal dates, price changes and any required confirmation follow the store's notices and subscription settings.

My membership → Manage subscription opens the relevant store settings. Cancellation generally stops the next renewal and retains access during the paid period, subject to store rules. Uninstalling, clearing app data or deleting a future account does not cancel store billing.

Request refunds through [Apple](https://support.apple.com/118223) or [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637?hl=en), subject to store rules and applicable law. FoodLens support can help investigate access problems. These terms do not limit statutory consumer or refund rights.

## Restoring purchases and identity

Restore on the original purchase platform with the same store account, subject to configured store and RevenueCat restoration and transfer rules. Restoration does not grant a fresh allowance for the same billing period.

The first version does not require a FoodLens registration. The server issues a random usage identity, whose credential is saved by the app and used for configured subscription verification. Anonymous Android and iOS identities do not automatically share subscriptions or synchronize photo history. A lost identity, reinstall or replacement device may require purchase restoration. Do not share your credential.

## Service and information handling

Pro increases real label-recognition allowance. It does not provide medical diagnosis, allergen safety certification, clinical validation or guaranteed accuracy. Check original packaging and seek professional medical advice. Recognition depends on networks, model availability and anti-abuse limits.

When real purchases are enabled, RevenueCat helps verify store transactions, renewals, expiration, refunds and entitlements. Purchase must use an active [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) and [Terms of Use](terms.html) reflecting the configured providers and processing. Label photos and dietary preferences are not sent as RevenueCat customer attributes.

Until operator identity and public contacts are confirmed, this document is a plan draft. Store products, service capacity, identity restoration and billing must be verified before purchase is enabled.
