## Recognition problems

Use a clear photo of the ingredients and nutrition panel. Keep the camera steady, avoid glare, and crop closely without removing serving sizes or allergen statements. If different panels are on different sides of a package, scan the relevant panel separately and check the original label before using the result.

The app compresses a cropped image before upload to keep text readable and reduce transfer size. Large image dimensions alone do not show that an upload failed. Connection errors, an unclear label, model availability, or a shared service limit may prevent a result. Try again after checking your network; avoid repeated rapid retries.

For help, email your app version, device model, operating-system version, and the error message. If you choose to attach a photo, remove personal information first. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or unnecessary health information. You may report an incorrect or concerning result to the same support address; include the field that was wrong and the label wording if possible.

## Privacy and deleting local data

Photos are sent for recognition only after the app's cloud-processing prompt is accepted. Camera permission and cloud-processing agreement are separate choices. Declining the prompt leaves example results available.

To stop future uploads, open My (我的) → Agreements and privacy (协议与隐私) → Turn off cloud recognition (关闭云端识别授权). Existing history remains and the next upload asks again. This does not retract already-sent requests.

To remove app-saved information, open My (我的), select Clear local data (清除本地数据), and confirm. This clears history, recognized text and values, thumbnails, favorites, preferences, the cloud-consent setting, and the legal-document acknowledgment. The onboarding-completed flag remains. It does not necessarily clear device-managed temporary files or backups, and it does not remove server usage counters.

FoodLens currently has no account and no remote history recovery. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for retention, cloud processing, and privacy rights. Contact the privacy address above for requests about information we hold.

## Payments

Purchases are not enabled in this preview. The new free-use identity has three successful recognitions in total. My → FoodLens Pro Monthly opens allowance and plan information. Clearing local history does not reset server allowance or clear the usage credential. If a future version offers a subscription, its purchase screen will show the store price, billing period, benefits, usage limits, and renewal conditions before confirmation. Store subscription settings control cancellation; deleting the app does not cancel store billing.

Verify original packaging and manufacturer allergen statements. No warning is not proof that a food is safe for you.
